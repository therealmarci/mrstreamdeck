# Keys for players

Every key shows a symbol and a title at the top, the value in the middle and a coloured stripe at the bottom: **green** all good, **yellow** watch out, **red** problem, **blue** note, **grey** off or nothing going on. Pressing opens the matching page of the web interface - except for keys that switch something.

| Key | Shows | Press |
|---|---|---|
| **Server overview** | players online / maximum; below a restart countdown or maintenance | start page |
| **Next restart** | countdown to the next restart, stop or maintenance | start page |
| **Inbox** | unread notifications (news, changelogs, answers) | start page |
| **Tickets** | your open tickets, blue on a new answer | your tickets |
| **Statistics** | one of your game statistics - which one, you choose in the settings of the key | statistics page |
| **Sleep vote** | at night: how many sleep / how many are needed (in your world) | start page |
| **Chunk loader** | state of one of your chunk loaders (on, off, locked …) and its size | **switches it on or off** |
| **Duel** | your last duel (win, loss, opponent, kit) or your record | your duels |
| **Plugin key** | any key a plugin offers - also of later plugins | depends on the key |

> [!TIP]
> Which chunk loader, or last duel versus record: choose it in the settings of the key under **Key**. "Automatic" takes the first one.

Chunk loader, duel and sleep keys only exist when the server has MRchunks, MRpvp or MRsleep.

## Stream Deck +
The **overview dial** shows server, performance (team), tickets, warnings (team), inbox and the next restart. **Turning** goes through them, **pressing** or **tapping** opens the page in the browser. Only the pages you may see appear.
